扑克账本隐私政策

更新日期：2026年11月23日｜生效日期：2026年11月23日

扑克账本（Android 包名：com.lihx.pokerbankroll）由本应用开发者运营，联系邮箱 lihx325@gmail.com。应用备案号：皖ICP备2026037021号-2A。当前版本没有账号、广告、用户追踪或内购。

你主动填写的场次、场所、账本、玩家称呼、金额、筹码、备注及设置保存在设备本地，不会发送到我们的服务器。你可在应用内查看、修改、删除记录。卸载和设备损坏可能导致本地数据丢失；请主动创建备份。系统备份及设备迁移受系统与用户设置控制。

位置属于敏感个人信息。仅在你使用附近场所功能并单独授权系统定位权限后，在本机匹配已保存的场所；不上传位置。拒绝定位仍可手动选择场所。可通过系统应用权限设置撤回定位授权。

Android 权限说明：ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION（精确／粗略前台定位，可选，仅用于附近场所匹配，拒绝后可手动选场所）；INTERNET（联网权限，仅用于获取参考汇率）；VIBRATE（振动权限，用于界面交互反馈）。

应用通过 Frankfurter（frankfurter.app，欧洲央行参考汇率）获取汇率。服务在境外，请求不携带你的账目或定位，服务方会接收处理请求所需的网络 IP 等连接信息。联网失败时使用本地已有汇率。

应用使用 React Native/Hermes、Expo 文件／SQLite／定位模块、Skia/Victory、Reanimated/Gesture Handler 和系统日期选择器提供界面、存储、权限、图表与交互功能。未启用广告、统计追踪、账号、推送或购买服务 SDK；这些组件的本应用用途为本地功能。

你主动创建备份、导出 CSV 或导入时，系统文件选择器／分享面板只处理你选择的文件。备份文件未加密，接收位置和分享对象由你决定。请保护文件中的金额、备注和玩家称呼。

设置中的“撤回隐私同意”会暂停应用服务并重新显示本告知，保留本地账目，不删除系统已授予的权限；可另行在系统设置管理权限。政策发生变化时重新告知。未成年人不属于本应用的服务对象。数据权利请求与投诉可通过上述邮箱联系，我们将在收到请求后的 15 个工作日内受理并回复。

Poker Bankroll Privacy Policy

Updated: November 23, 2026 | Effective: November 23, 2026

Poker Bankroll (Android package: com.lihx.pokerbankroll) is operated by the developer of Poker Bankroll. Contact: lihx325@gmail.com. App filing: 皖ICP备2026037021号-2A. This version has no accounts, advertising, tracking or purchases.

Sessions, venues, table ledgers, player labels, amounts, stacks, notes and preferences you enter stay in local device storage. They are not sent to our servers. You can view, edit and delete records in the app. Uninstallation or device failure may cause loss; create backups. System backup and device transfer follow your system settings.

Location is sensitive information. Nearby saved venues are matched on-device only after separate system location permission. Location is not uploaded. You can refuse permission and select venues manually, and revoke permission in system settings.

Android permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (optional foreground location, used only for on-device nearby venue matching; manual selection works if denied); INTERNET (network access, used only to fetch reference exchange rates); VIBRATE (haptic feedback).

Exchange rates come from Frankfurter (frankfurter.app, ECB reference rates), a service outside mainland China. Requests contain no records or location; the provider receives connection information such as your IP address. Cached rates remain available if the network fails.

React Native/Hermes, Expo file/SQLite/location modules, Skia/Victory, Reanimated/Gesture Handler and the system date picker provide local interface, storage, permissions, charts and interactions. Advertising, analytics, account, push and purchase services are not enabled.

Backup, CSV export and import use the system picker or sharing interface for files you select. Backup files are unencrypted. You choose storage and recipients; protect the amounts, notes and player labels they contain.

Withdraw privacy consent in Settings to pause services and reopen this notice. Records are kept; existing system permissions can be managed separately. Policy changes require a new notice. The app is intended for adults. Contact the address above for privacy questions or data-rights requests; we will respond within 15 business days.
